Payment Methods

Cash on Delivery (COD)

Pay in cash when you receive your order at your doorstep:

  • Our courier will deliver your parcel directly to your address
  • Before making payment, verify your order number, sender information, and tracking number on the parcel
  • Pay the exact amount in cash to our courier upon delivery
  • Get your receipt and enjoy your purchase!

eSewa Payment

Complete your payment securely through eSewa:

  • Click "Pay Now" and you'll be redirected to the eSewa payment gateway
  • Login to your eSewa account using your eSewa ID and Password
  • Ensure your eSewa account has sufficient balance for the transaction
  • Review the payment details and confirm the transaction
  • Once payment is successful, you'll receive a confirmation message
  • Your order will be processed immediately after payment verification
